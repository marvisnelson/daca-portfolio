# Week 1: SQL Basics — Exploring UrbanStyle's data

## What I did
- Investigated the sales channels and locations within the sales table using SQL queries
- Found that sales operate across two primary channels (Online and Shop) with store locations in Tallinn, Pärnu, and Tartu, alongside three payment options (Cash, Card, and Installments)
- Took part in building the team's data landscape

## Key takeaways
- Online transactions exhibit a remarkably uniform pattern, showing a consistent purchase quantity of 5 across records
- A major data anomaly was identified: 10,408 transactions have no store location attached (NULL), representing a critical gap in physical store tracking
