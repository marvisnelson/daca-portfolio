# Tallinn Store Data Story — UrbanStyle Flagship Performance

Tallinn functions as UrbanStyle's primary revenue engine and flagship hub, achieving *€1.09M* in total revenue across *3,801 orders* with an average order value (AOV) of *€287.53*.

---

### Key Revenue Drivers & Seasonality
Monthly performance demonstrated strong elasticity throughout the year, culminating in a record peak of *€110,426.51* in December—a *34% rebound* over the October baseline of *€82,561.84*. This year-end surge underscores the high effectiveness of targeted Q4 holiday promotional campaigns in activating retail demand.

---

### Product Anchors & Customer Loyalty
Product performance is anchored by core bestsellers, led by **Praktiline džersii seelik*, which generated *€10,540.86** as the store's single highest revenue-generating item. Furthermore, customer lifetime value is heavily concentrated among registered brand advocates, with *Silver* and *Gold* loyalty tiers driving over *€505K* in total sales.
