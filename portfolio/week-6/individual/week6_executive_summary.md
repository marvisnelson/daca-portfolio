Executive Summary — Tallinn Flagship Store Performance
​Overview
​As UrbanStyle's flagship and top-performing retail location, the Tallinn store generated €1.09M in overall revenue across 3,801 transactions, maintaining a strong Average Order Value (AOV) of €287.53.


​Key Strategic Findings
​📈 Holiday Demand Surge:

​Finding: Monthly sales reached a record peak of €110.4K in December, marking a 34% recovery from October sales (€82.6K).

​So What? High-impact seasonal promotional campaigns serve as UrbanStyle's primary mechanism for short-term revenue acceleration.

​🛍️️ Hero Product Revenue Anchor:

​Finding: Praktiline džersii seelik stands out as the #1 bestseller, generating €10.5K in revenue.

​So What? Key hero items serve as critical store traffic anchors and provide prime cross-selling opportunities for complementary items.

​👥 Loyalty-Driven Revenue Core:
​Finding: High-tier loyalty members (Silver and Gold tiers) account for the vast majority of store sales, contributing over €505K.

​So What? Tallinn operates primarily as a customer retention powerhouse rather than relying on cold foot traffic, serving as a blueprint for customer lifetime value (LTV).


​Actionable Recommendations
​Replicate Holiday Frameworks: Institutionalize the Q4 promotional playbook for execution across all regional store hubs.

​Product Bundling: Pair top-performing items like Praktiline džersii seelik with slower-moving inventory to raise average order values.

​Export VIP Playbooks: Scale Tallinn's VIP loyalty engagement model to secondary store locations to drive network-wide retention.
