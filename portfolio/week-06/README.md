# Week 6 — Tallinn Store Dashboard + Narrative

## Executive Overview
This portfolio project delivers an end-to-end performance analysis and Power BI dashboard for UrbanStyle's *Tallinn Flagship Store. The project translates raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights using key data visualization and storytelling best practices from **Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic* (Storytelling with Data).

## Core Performance Metrics (Tallinn Store)

* *Total Revenue:* €1,092,829.35 (€1.09M) — Primary revenue contributor across the UrbanStyle retail network.
* *Total Orders:* 3,801 transactions — High transaction volume sustained across all four quarters.
* *Average Order Value (AOV):* €287.53 — Reflects strong customer basket size and effective multi-item purchasing.

---

## Key Business Insights

1. *Q4 Demand Surge:*
   * Tallinn sales reached a peak of *€110.4K* in December, representing a *34% growth* compared to October (€82.6K).
   * Business Impact: Proves the success of Q4 promotional strategies and highlights seasonal holiday demand as UrbanStyle's strongest growth lever.

2. *Top Product Anchor & Category Performers:*
   * Praktiline džersii seelik generated *€10.5K* in total sales, leading the top 5 product category anchors highlighted on the store bar chart.
   * Business Impact: Serves as the primary store entry driver and key candidate for strategic cross-selling and bundling campaigns.

3. *Loyalty Program Value:*
   * Registered brand members (*Silver* and *Gold* tiers) generated over *€505K* in total revenue.
   * Business Impact: Demonstrates that Tallinn relies heavily on customer retention and high lifetime value rather than purely cold foot traffic.

---

## Methodology & Visualization Best Practices

Applied core principles from *Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic* (Storytelling with Data):
* *Decluttering & Focus:* Removed non-essential gridlines and formatted key callout numbers to minimize cognitive load for executive reviewers.
* *Pipelined Context & Annotations:* Added embedded text annotations directly onto line and bar charts to immediately answer why revenue spikes occurred in Q4 and draw direct focus to the top 5 revenue-generating products.
