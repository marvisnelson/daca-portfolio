# Week 6 — UrbanStyle Multi-Location Combined Performance View

## Executive Summary
This combined performance view integrates data insights across all four UrbanStyle retail channels: *Tallinn (Flagship Store), **Online Store, **Tartu, and **Pärnu*. By synthesizing cross-channel trends, the team identified key growth drivers, debunked operational assumptions, and established unified strategic priorities for the entire network.

## Detailed Channel Breakdowns

### Role A — Tallinn Flagship Store
* *Core Performance:* Tallinn remains UrbanStyle's primary revenue engine, generating over *€1.09M* across *3,801 orders*.
* *Key Insights:* Sales reached a seasonal peak of *€110,426.51 in December, rebounding sharply from an October baseline of €82,561.84. Sales are heavily anchored by core SKUs such as *Praktiline džersii seelik (€10,540.86) and sustained by high-tier (Silver and Gold) loyalty members.
* *Strategic Play:* Export Tallinn's VIP loyalty retention playbooks and product-bundling strategies to secondary regional hubs to drive network-wide retention.

### Role D — Online Store
* *Core Performance:* Online generated *€925.18K* across 3,191 orders (2023–2024 combined) with an Average Order Value (AOV) of *€289.94*.
* *Key Insights:* Revenue grew *37.9% YoY* in 2024, surging from €388.97K (2023) to *€536.21K (2024). December marked the single highest sales month at *€63.2K**, with the top individual SKU generating €13.9K.
* *Strategic Play:* Identify specific customer behaviors and hero products driving the December peak, and run targeted tests to replicate these digital conversion patterns year-round.

### Role B — Tartu Store
* *Core Performance:* Total combined revenue stands at *€489.36K, showing a **13% YoY increase* from €229K (2023) to €260K (2024).
* *Key Insights:* Data directly refutes initial assumptions of a declining trend. Growth was volume-driven—orders increased by *16%* and purchasing customers grew by *18%, offsetting a minor **3% decrease in AOV (€287)*. Performance remains volatile month-to-month (e.g., April down -31% YoY, followed by May up +97% YoY).
* *Strategic Play:* Re-evaluate operations to treat Tartu as a growth channel rather than a underperforming store, focusing on smoothing monthly volatility and protecting average basket sizes.

### Role C — Pärnu Store
* *Core Performance:* Pärnu generated *€272.87K–€288.74K* from 747 customers, representing UrbanStyle's smallest physical presence.
* *Key Insights:* Shows a moderate summer uptick—August peaked near *€33K* compared to a *€24K monthly average, while May experienced a soft point at *~€19.5K**. Because top sellers include cooler-weather items, Pärnu displays steady baseline performance rather than dramatic resort-town seasonality.
* *Strategic Play:* Implement targeted spring promotional activities to lift May performance, treating Pärnu as a consistent secondary retail hub rather than relying purely on summer tourism.

---

## Unified Strategic Recommendations

1. *Scale Online Operations:* Capitalize on digital momentum (+37.9% YoY) as UrbanStyle's fastest-growing channel.
2. *Fund & Replicate Tallinn's Loyalty Program:* Leverage Tallinn’s high-margin Silver/Gold retention strategy across other regional locations.
3. *Reframe Tartu Strategy:* Stop treating Tartu as a declining asset; support its high customer volume acquisition with strategies to stabilize monthly swings.
4. *Targeted Support for Pärnu:* Maintain light, strategic promotions during off-peak windows (such as May) without overhauling seasonal operational budgets.
