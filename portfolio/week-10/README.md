# 10-Week Data Analytics Journey: AI-Driven Workflow & Team Collaboration

Welcome to the repository documentation! Over the past 10 weeks, our team completed an intensive data analytics program focused on building end-to-end pipelines—ranging from raw data extraction and database queries to automated scripts, customer segmentation, business intelligence dashboards, and final executive presentations for **UrbanStyle**.

As the team member who felt most confident leveraging AI, I took the lead in integrating artificial intelligence as an operational force multiplier across our tech stack. Below is an overview of how we utilized AI, why it mattered, our team roles, and an honest reflection on the balance between automated acceleration and human critical thinking.

---

## 🚀 How We Used AI Over the 10 Weeks

AI served as an intelligent technical partner throughout our data lifecycle—accelerating code execution, debugging syntax errors, and streamlining our overall delivery. 

### Concrete Examples
1. **Debugging & Code Generation:** 
   * During early database exploration, AI assisted in optimizing multi-table SQL queries, resolving complex join logic, and refactoring data cleaning scripts.
   * As we transitioned to Python, it accelerated script development for our automated REST API pipelines, error handling, and vectorized Pandas data transformations.
2. **Understanding Data & Logic Validation:** 
   * AI served as a valuable logic check when validating our RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) customer segmentation model and exploring dataset trends.
3. **Preparing the Presentation:** 
   * AI assisted in structuring our business intelligence findings into executive storytelling frameworks for our final board meeting presentation.

### Honest Reflection: Where AI Helped vs. Where We Thought It Through
* **Where AI helped the most:** AI proved invaluable as an execution accelerator. It drastically reduced time spent hunting down syntax errors, drafted baseline automation scripts, and helped us quickly format complex data transformations.
* **Where we had to think it through ourselves:** AI lacks business domain awareness and deep context. While it generated code and suggested initial logic, our team had to verify schema accuracy, audit math models, and ensure that every final analytical output and business recommendation aligned directly with UrbanStyle’s unique strategy. **Human-in-the-loop verification** was entirely our responsibility.
